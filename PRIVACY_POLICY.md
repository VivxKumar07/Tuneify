# 🛡️ Privacy Policy for Tuneify

**Last Updated: 2026**

## Introduction

**Tuneify** ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application functions and handles your data.

## Information Handling

### 1. Account & Preferences
- **Local Storage**: Your listening preferences, favorites, playlists, and cached tracks are stored securely on your local device.
- **Third-Party Logins**: If you choose to log in with your YouTube or Spotify accounts, authentication is handled securely via standard session cookies directly with the respective service.

### 2. Music Streaming
- Tuneify streams audio directly from public content delivery networks. We do not operate external intermediate servers that log your playback activity or track identity.

### 3. Analytics & Diagnostics
- Anonymized crash logs may be processed to identify and resolve software bugs and improve app stability.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

## Permissions Requested
- **Audio & Media**: Required to discover and play audio files stored locally on your device.
- **Notifications**: Required to display media player playback controls and background service indicators.
- **Network Access**: Required to stream audio, retrieve lyrics, and download covers.
